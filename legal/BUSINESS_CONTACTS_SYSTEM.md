## 📊 **Contact Information Template**

### **Basic Information**
- **Name:** Full name and title
- **Company:** Organization name
- **Role:** Job title and responsibilities
- **Department:** Specific department or division

### **Contact Details**
- **Email:** Primary email address
- **Phone:** Business phone number
- **Mobile:** Mobile phone (if provided)
- **Address:** Business address
- **Website:** Company website

### **Business Context**
- **Relationship:** Type of business relationship
- **Services:** Services provided or received
- **Status:** Active, Inactive, Prospect, etc.
- **Priority:** High, Medium, Low
- **Notes:** Additional context and information

### **Communication History**
- **Last Contact:** Date of last communication
- **Next Follow-up:** Scheduled follow-up date
- **Communication Type:** Email, Phone, Meeting, etc.
- **Outcome:** Result of communication
- **Documents:** Related files and attachments

## 🎯 **Contact Management Workflow**

### **Adding New Contacts**
1. **Gather Information** - Collect all available details
2. **Create Profile** - Use the contact template
3. **Categorize** - Assign appropriate category
4. **Set Priority** - Determine importance level
5. **Schedule Follow-up** - Plan next contact
6. **Document** - Save all related communications

### **Maintaining Contacts**
1. **Regular Updates** - Keep information current
2. **Track Communications** - Log all interactions
3. **Follow-up Reminders** - Schedule next contacts
4. **Document Management** - Organize related files
5. **Relationship Building** - Nurture business relationships

### **Contact Categories**

2. **Active Clients** - Current business relationships
