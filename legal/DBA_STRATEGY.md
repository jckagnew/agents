## 📋 **DBA Registration Process**

### **Texas Assumed Name Certificates**
**Filing Requirements:**
- **Form:** Assumed Name Certificate
- **Filing Fee:** $25 per DBA
- **Total Cost:** $75 (3 DBAs)
- **Validity:** 10 years
- **Renewal:** Required every 10 years

### **Filing Process**
1. **Complete Forms:** Fill out 3 separate Assumed Name Certificates
2. **Submit to Texas Secretary of State**
3. **Pay Filing Fees:** $25 × 3 = $75
4. **Processing Time:** 5-7 business days
5. **Receive Certificates:** Official DBA registrations
