## ✅ **Entry Checklist**

Before adding an expense, verify:
- [ ] **Date is correct** - Use actual purchase date
- [ ] **Description is clear** - What was purchased
- [ ] **Vendor is accurate** - Company name
- [ ] **Amount is correct** - Include tax if applicable
- [ ] **Category is appropriate** - Use guide above
- [ ] **Receipt is referenced** - Invoice number or receipt
- [ ] **Status is current** - Paid/Pending/Reimbursed

## 💡 **Pro Tips**

### **Receipt Management**
- **Take photos** of physical receipts
- **Save digital receipts** in organized folders
- **Name files clearly** - Date_Vendor_Amount
- **Backup everything** - Multiple locations

### **Tax Preparation**
- **Categorize correctly** - Helps with deductions
- **Keep detailed records** - IRS requirements
- **Separate personal/business** - Clear boundaries
- **Review quarterly** - Catch errors early

### **Budget Control**
- **Set monthly limits** - Prevent overspending
- **Track by category** - Identify trends
- **Review regularly** - Adjust as needed
- **Plan ahead** - Anticipate large expenses
