# Weight Tracker Collaboration – Pre-Sign Checklist

Use this to confirm all business and legal terms are ready before sending the joint venture agreement to Alex for execution.

## Commercial Terms
- [ ] Product name and description confirmed (`Weight Tracker Next.js app + backend`).
- [ ] Profit split agreed (currently 50/50 net).  
- [ ] Definition of “Net Profits” drafted (deductions, hosting, marketing, payment fees).  
- [ ] Responsibility matrix drafted (development: Splendid Software; design/product: Alex).  
- [ ] Ownership of IP clarified (LLC owns; Alex grants license to his assets or assigns outright).  
- [ ] Launch plan & monetization model documented (subscriptions, marketplace, etc.).

## Legal Inputs

- [ ] Confidentiality/NDA signed (if not already).  
- [ ] Access credentials, repositories, and shared tools enumerated.

## Operations

- [ ] Repository permissions set (GitHub, Vercel, Supabase, etc.).

- [ ] Support & maintenance expectations documented (response times, bug triage).

## Post-Sign Actions
