This Operating Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on `[EFFECTIVE DATE]` by John David Agnew ("Member"), the sole member of C-Level Sales Guy LLC, a Texas limited liability company (the "Company").

## 1. Formation
1.1 **Certificate of Formation.** The Member has caused the Company to be formed by filing a Certificate of Formation with the Texas Secretary of State pursuant to the Texas Business Organizations Code (“TBOC”).  
1.2 **Principal Office.** The Company's principal business address is 1122 Jackson St., APT 808, Dallas, TX 75202, or such other address as the Member designates.  
1.3 **Registered Agent.** Northwest Registered Agent, LLC., 5900 Balcones Drive STE 100, Austin, TX 78731.

## 2. Purpose
The Company’s purpose is to develop, market, license, and support software products and services, operate a software development studio, and engage in any other lawful business in Texas or elsewhere.

## 3. Member; Ownership
3.1 **Sole Member.** John David Agnew is the Company's sole member and owns 100% of the Membership Interest.  
3.2 **Capital Contributions.** Initial contribution: $500.00 cash contribution and $1,000.00 non-cash contribution (intellectual property assets as detailed in Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement). Additional contributions may be made at the Member's discretion.  
3.3 **Additional Members.** No additional members may be admitted without the Member’s written consent and adoption of an amended agreement.

## 4. Management
4.1 **Member-Managed.** The Company is managed by the Member, who has full authority to bind the Company.  
4.2 **Officers.** The Member may appoint officers (e.g., President, CTO) and specify their powers in written resolutions.  
4.3 **Decision-Making.** All decisions rest with the Member unless delegated in writing.

## 5. Intellectual Property & Work Product
5.1 **Assignment to Company.** The Member hereby assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest in software, inventions, trademarks, domain names, documentation, designs, marketing materials, and other intellectual property created before or after formation that relate to the Company's business. This assignment is documented in the Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement executed contemporaneously with this Operating Agreement.  
5.2 **Third-Party Contractors.** The Member shall secure written agreements with contractors and collaborators assigning IP and ensuring confidentiality for deliverables created for the Company.  
5.3 **Use of Personal Branding.** The Member grants the Company a nonexclusive license to reference "C-Level Sales Guy" or other personal branding for marketing the Company's products, subject to Member approval.

## 6. Finances
6.1 **Fiscal Year & Accounting.** Fiscal year ends December 31, unless changed by the Member. Books are kept on an accrual or cash basis (select one).  
6.2 **Banking.** Company funds shall be held in separate bank accounts. The Member is the authorized signatory.  
6.3 **Distributions.** Distributions are made at the Member’s discretion, subject to TBOC limitations on wrongful distributions.  
6.4 **Taxes.** The Company will be treated as a disregarded entity for U.S. federal tax purposes unless the Member elects otherwise.

## 7. Liability & Indemnification
7.1 **Limited Liability.** The Member’s liability is limited to the extent provided by law.  
7.2 **Indemnification.** The Company shall indemnify the Member and duly appointed officers against claims arising from Company activities, except for gross negligence, willful misconduct, or knowing law violations.

## 8. Records & Reporting
8.1 Maintain records required by TBOC, including: Certificate of Formation, minutes/resolutions, tax returns, financial statements, contracts, and IP assignments.  
8.2 Keep records at the principal office or in a secure digital repository accessible to the Member.

## 9. Dissolution
9.1 The Company dissolves upon: (a) Member decision; (b) sale of substantially all assets; (c) entry of a decree of judicial dissolution.  
9.2 Upon dissolution, wind up affairs, satisfy creditors, and distribute remaining assets to the Member.  
9.3 File a Certificate of Termination with the Texas Secretary of State.

## 10. Miscellaneous
10.1 **Amendments.** Only by written instrument signed by the Member.  
10.2 **Governing Law.** Texas law.  
10.3 **Severability.** Invalid provisions will be modified or severed to preserve intent.  
10.4 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed electronically.
