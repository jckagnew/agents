# INTERNAL IP & CONFIDENTIALITY POLICY (DRAFT)

> Intended for everyone working with Splendid Software, LLC (employees, contractors, collaborators). Use as a starting point for an employee handbook or onboarding packet. Review with counsel.

## 1. Purpose
Protect Splendid Software’s intellectual property, confidential information, and customer data; clarify ownership and handling expectations for anyone performing work for the company.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all staff, contractors, vendors, and joint-venture collaborators who access company systems, source code, documentation, analytics, or customer information.

## 3. Confidential Information
- Includes source code, prototypes, product roadmaps, customer lists, pricing, internal tools, analytics, financial data, and any non-public business information.  
- Treat all confidential information as company property. Do not copy, disclose, or use it for any purpose other than company-approved work.  
- Use company-approved storage (e.g., GitHub private repos, secure cloud drives). No personal Dropbox/Google Drive unless approved.

## 4. Intellectual Property
- All work product created while engaged by Splendid Software (including code, designs, documents, analytics models, marketing copy) belongs to Splendid under the terms of the governing contract (employment agreement, contractor agreement, JV).  
- Contributors must sign an IP assignment or work-for-hire agreement before accessing repositories.  
- Use open-source or third-party libraries only with proper licenses; document usage in project repos.

## 5. Access & Security
- Use unique credentials; enable MFA wherever possible.  
- Do not share passwords or tokens. Rotate credentials when collaborators leave.  
- Report lost devices, suspected breaches, or misdirected emails immediately to `[SECURITY CONTACT]`.  
- Follow the Universal Verification Framework for testing before deployments.

## 6. Data Handling
- Limit access to customer data to those with a business need.  
- Anonymize or pseudonymize data when exporting outside production environments.  
- Follow applicable privacy policies and legal requirements (GDPR/CPRA) when handling personal data.

## 7. Third-Party Engagements
- Ensure contractors and JV partners execute NDAs and IP assignment agreements before sharing proprietary information.  
- All subcontracting must be approved in writing. Subcontractors must agree to these same obligations.

## 8. Departure & Termination
- Upon termination or completion of work, all confidential materials must be returned or deleted.  
- Access credentials (GitHub, Supabase, Vercel, analytics tools) must be revoked within 24 hours.  
- Off-boarding checklist should be filed with operations.

## 9. Compliance & Enforcement
- Violations may result in contract termination, legal action, or other remedies available under law.  
- Questions should be directed to `[OPERATIONS/LEGAL CONTACT]`.  
- This policy may be updated; continued engagement signifies acceptance of current terms.
