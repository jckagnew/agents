## 1. Purpose
Develop, launch, and commercialize the Weight Tracker software solution (“Product”), combining Alex’s product concept and design assets with Splendid’s software development and operational capability.

## 2. Roles & Responsibilities
2.1 **Splendid:** Software architecture, coding, testing, deployment, hosting, customer support, analytics, compliance, and ongoing maintenance.  
2.2 **Alex:** Product requirements, user experience and visual design, domain expertise, marketing collateral (if any), and customer discovery support.  
2.3 **Decision Process:** Strategic product decisions require mutual written approval. Day-to-day technical decisions rest with Splendid; design decisions rest with Alex, subject to feasibility.  
2.4 **Time Commitment:** Each Party will invest reasonable professional effort to achieve milestones in Exhibit A (Statement of Work).

## 3. Intellectual Property
3.1 **Ownership.** All right, title, and interest in the Product, code, databases, documentation, marketing assets, and derivatives (excluding Alex’s pre-existing materials) are owned by Splendid.  
3.2 **License to Alex’s Assets.** Alex grants Splendid a perpetual, irrevocable license (or assignment, if preferred) to use, modify, and commercialize the design assets he provides for the Product.  
3.3 **Joint Branding.** Public materials may reference “Weight Tracker by Splendid Software in collaboration with Alex Biggus”; final branding to be mutually agreed.  
3.4 **Use Outside Agreement.** Neither Party may use the Product IP outside the Agreement without written consent of the other Party.

## 4. Profit Sharing
4.1 **Revenue Definition.** “Gross Revenue” means all collected revenue from the Product (subscriptions, licenses, services), excluding sales tax and refunds.  
4.2 **Expense Deduction.** “Net Profits” = Gross Revenue minus mutually approved, documented expenses (hosting, third-party APIs, marketing spend, payment processing fees, taxes).  
4.3 **Split.** Net Profits are distributed 50% to Splendid and 50% to Alex within `[X]` days after the close of each calendar month/quarter.  
4.4 **Accounting.** Splendid maintains accurate financial records and provides Alex a profit & loss statement with each payout. Alex may audit once per year upon reasonable notice.  
4.5 **Losses.** Financial deficits are borne by Splendid unless the Parties agree in writing to share specific costs.

## 5. Term; Termination
5.1 **Term.** Begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated under this section.  
5.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate on 60 days’ written notice after the Product’s initial launch.  
5.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate if the other commits a material breach and fails to cure within 30 days.  
5.4 **Effect of Termination.** Splendid retains Product ownership. Alex’s future revenue share ends upon termination, except for amounts accrued but unpaid. If termination was without cause by Splendid, consider a negotiated buyout or royalty tail (insert terms if desired).  
5.5 **Survival.** Payment obligations, confidentiality, IP rights, and dispute resolution survive termination.

## 6. Confidentiality
6.1 Each Party agrees to protect confidential information of the other, limit use to project purposes, and return/destroy materials upon request.  
6.2 Optional: incorporate or reference an existing NDA.

## 7. Representations & Warranties
Each Party represents it has authority to sign; will not knowingly infringe third-party IP; and will comply with applicable laws. No other warranties are made; the Product is provided “as is” between the Parties.

## 8. Liability
Neither Party is liable to the other for special or consequential damages arising from this Agreement, except for breaches of confidentiality, IP misuse, or gross negligence/willful misconduct.

## 9. Dispute Resolution
9.1 **Governing Law.** Texas law, without regard to conflicts principles.  
9.2 **Venue.** State or federal courts located in Dallas County, Texas.  
9.3 **Negotiation / Mediation.** Parties will first meet to negotiate in good faith; if unresolved, consider mediation before litigation.

## 10. Miscellaneous
- Amendments must be in writing signed by both Parties.  
- Neither Party may assign rights without the other’s consent (except Splendid may assign to an affiliate or successor).  
- Notices must be sent to the addresses listed above (email notice acceptable if receipt acknowledged).
