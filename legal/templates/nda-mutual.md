# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA)

> Draft for review; covers two-way confidentiality ahead of collaborations.

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into as of `[EFFECTIVE DATE]` by and between Splendid Software, LLC, a Texas limited liability company (“Party A”), and `[COUNTERPARTY NAME]`, a `[STATE] [ENTITY TYPE]` (“Party B”).

## 1. Purpose
The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship regarding `[PROJECT/TRANSACTION]` and may exchange certain confidential information (“Purpose”).

## 2. Confidential Information
“Confidential Information” means any non-public business, technical, financial, or other information disclosed by either Party, whether oral, written, electronic, or otherwise, that is designated as confidential or reasonably should be understood as confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes, without limitation, product roadmaps, source code, prototypes, designs, specifications, customer data, pricing, marketing plans, and trade secrets.

## 3. Obligations
Each Party will:  
1. use the other Party’s Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;  
2. not disclose it to third parties except to employees, contractors, or advisors who need to know and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective; and  
3. exercise reasonable care (no less than used to protect its own confidential information) to safeguard it.

## 4. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) becomes public through no breach; (b) was known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure; (c) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party’s Confidential Information; or (d) is obtained lawfully from a third party without confidentiality obligations.

## 5. Required Disclosure
If a receiving Party is compelled by law or court order to disclose Confidential Information, it will provide prompt notice (unless legally prohibited) and cooperate with any lawful efforts to limit or protect the disclosure.

## 6. No License
No rights or licenses to trademarks, patents, copyrights, or other intellectual property are granted by this Agreement. Confidential Information remains the property of the disclosing Party.

## 7. Term & Termination
This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and remains in force for one (1) year, unless extended in writing. Each Party’s confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years after termination, except trade secrets, which remain protected as long as they qualify as trade secrets under applicable law.

## 8. Return or Destruction
Upon written request, the receiving Party will promptly destroy or return Confidential Information, except that one archival copy may be kept for legal compliance.

## 9. No Warranty
All Confidential Information is provided “as is” without warranties.

## 10. Governing Law
Texas law governs this Agreement. Venue is in state or federal courts located in Dallas County, Texas.

## 11. Miscellaneous
- This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding regarding confidential disclosures for the Purpose.  
- Amendments must be in writing signed by both Parties.  
- Neither Party may assign without the other’s written consent, except to an affiliate or successor.  
- Counterparts and electronic signatures are valid.
