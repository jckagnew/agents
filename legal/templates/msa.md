# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (MSA)

> Draft for Splendid Software, LLC. Adapt per engagement; obtain attorney review.

This Master Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is effective as of `[EFFECTIVE DATE]` between:
- **Splendid Software, LLC**, a Texas limited liability company (“Provider”), and
- `[CLIENT LEGAL NAME]`, a `[STATE] [ENTITY TYPE]` (“Client”).

Provider and Client each may be referred to as a “Party” and together as the “Parties.”

## 1. Scope of Services
Provider will deliver software development, integration, consulting, or related services described in one or more Statements of Work (“SOWs”) executed under this Agreement. Each SOW is incorporated herein.

## 2. Term
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated as outlined in Section 10.

## 3. Fees & Payment
- Fees, billing schedule, and expenses are set forth in the applicable SOW.  
- Client reimburses pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses (travel, third-party licenses).  
- Invoices are due within `[NET 15/30]` days. Late payments accrue interest at `[1.5% per month]` (or the maximum allowed by law).  
- Provider may pause work for invoices more than `[X]` days overdue.

## 4. Client Responsibilities
- Provide timely access to information, personnel, and systems needed for the Services.  
- Ensure third-party assets supplied to Provider are appropriately licensed and free of infringement.  
- Designate a project manager for approvals and clarifications.

## 5. Intellectual Property
5.1 **Provider Materials.** Provider retains ownership of pre-existing tools, frameworks, libraries, and know-how (“Provider IP”).  
5.2 **Deliverables.** Upon Client’s full payment, Deliverables are assigned to Client subject to Section 5.3.  
5.3 **License Back.** Provider receives a non-exclusive, perpetual license to use generalized learnings, non-proprietary components, and residual knowledge (excluding Client confidential data) for other projects.  
5.4 **Open Source.** Provider may incorporate open-source software under standard licenses disclosed in the Deliverables.

## 6. Confidentiality
Each Party will keep the other’s Confidential Information safe and use it only to perform obligations under this Agreement. Disclosure is permitted to the receiving Party’s employees, contractors, and advisors who have a need to know and are bound by similar confidentiality obligations.

## 7. Warranties & Disclaimer
- Provider warrants that Services will be performed in a professional manner consistent with industry standards.  
- Provider does not warrant that Deliverables will be error-free or achieve specific business results.  
- EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED, THE SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES ARE PROVIDED “AS IS.” IMPLIED WARRANTIES (MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS, NON-INFRINGEMENT) ARE DISCLAIMED.

## 8. Indemnification
- Provider will indemnify Client against third-party claims alleging the Deliverables infringe IP, provided Client promptly notifies Provider and allows Provider control of the defense.  
- Client will indemnify Provider against claims arising from Client data, instructions, or misuse of Deliverables.  
- Indemnification obligations do not apply if the claim arises from modifications not authorized by the indemnifying Party or combination with items not supplied by that Party.

## 9. Limitation of Liability
Neither Party is liable to the other for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. Aggregate liability of each Party under this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid or payable during the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, except for payment obligations, IP infringement indemnity, or breach of confidentiality.

## 10. Termination
- Either Party may terminate for convenience with 30 days’ written notice.  
- Either Party may terminate for material breach if not cured within 15 days after notice.  
- Client shall pay for Services performed and expenses incurred through termination date.  
- Sections 5–11 survive termination.

## 11. General
- **Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor; nothing creates a partnership or employment relationship.  
- **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign without the other’s consent, except Provider may assign to an affiliate or successor in merger.  
- **Governing Law.** Texas law; venue in Dallas County (state or federal courts).  
- **Notices.** Sent to the addresses in the SOW via email with receipt confirmation or certified mail.  
- **Entire Agreement.** This MSA plus SOWs constitutes the entire agreement.
