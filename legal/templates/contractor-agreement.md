# INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

> Use for collaborators you pay directly (not profit-sharing). Review with counsel.

This Independent Contractor Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into as of `[EFFECTIVE DATE]` between:
- **Splendid Software, LLC**, a Texas limited liability company (“Company”), and
- `[CONTRACTOR NAME]`, an independent contractor with principal address `[ADDRESS]` (“Contractor”).

## 1. Services
Contractor will perform the services described in Exhibit A (“Services”) on a non-exclusive basis, using Contractor’s own tools and methods, and deliver the deliverables described therein.

## 2. Compensation
Company will pay Contractor `[RATE/FLAT FEE]` as outlined in Exhibit A. Contractor is responsible for all taxes, insurance, and benefits. Invoices are due `[NET TERMS]` days from receipt.

## 3. Term & Termination
This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until the Services are completed, unless terminated earlier. Either Party may terminate with 14 days’ written notice. Company may terminate immediately for material breach or misconduct. Contractor will be paid for Services performed and approved expenses incurred through termination.

## 4. Independent Contractor Status
Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee. Nothing herein creates a partnership, joint venture, or employment relationship. Contractor has no authority to bind Company.

## 5. Confidentiality
Contractor will keep Company’s Confidential Information confidential, use it only to perform the Services, and return or destroy it upon request. Consider attaching or referencing the mutual NDA.

## 6. Intellectual Property; Work Made for Hire
6.1 All deliverables, work product, inventions, designs, code, documentation, and other materials prepared by Contractor in connection with the Services (“Work Product”) are “work made for hire” for Company to the maximum extent permitted by law.  
6.2 To the extent not work made for hire, Contractor assigns to Company all rights, title, and interest in the Work Product.  
6.3 Contractor will sign additional documents reasonably requested to confirm such ownership.  
6.4 Contractor retains no license or rights in the Work Product except an internal portfolio reference with Company’s written permission.

## 7. Representations & Warranties
Contractor represents that: (a) the Work Product is original and does not infringe third-party rights; (b) Contractor will comply with applicable laws; (c) Contractor holds all necessary licenses. Company provides no warranty to Contractor.

## 8. Indemnification
Contractor will indemnify Company against claims arising from Contractor’s breach of this Agreement, negligence, or infringement of third-party rights.

## 9. Non-Solicitation (Optional)
For `[12]` months after termination, Contractor will not solicit Company employees or clients they interacted with during the engagement, except with Company’s written consent.

## 10. General Provisions
- Governing law: Texas; venue in Dallas County courts.  
- Entire agreement: This Agreement plus exhibits.  
- Amendments: Written, signed by both Parties.  
- Assignment: Contractor may not assign without Company consent. Company may assign to an affiliate or successor.  
- Notices: Email with receipt confirmation or certified mail to addresses above.  
- Counterparts/e-signatures permitted.
